# Best practices for preparing presentations

**Relevance and Accessibility of Content**

* The presentation is informative and straightforward to understand, avoiding overly technical jargon.
* Focuses on educating attendees regarding broader practices rather than deep technical details.

**Well-Formed Outline**

* The outline logically structures the presentation, starting from foundational concepts and building towards integrated discussions.
* Includes an introduction, well-defined sections with cohesive transitions, and a conclusion that reinforces key takeaways.

**Engagement and Interactivity**

* Incorporates interactive elements such as Q&A, polls, or group discussions to engage the audience and enhance the learning experience.

#### Comprehensive Coverage

* Ensures all relevant aspects of the topic are covered comprehensively, providing a holistic understanding.
* Balances depth and breadth to cater to both novice and experienced participants.
* Check for gaps in topic coverage that leave attendees with incomplete understanding.
* Ensure topics are sufficient to give an overview, yet detailed sufficiently to provide actionable insights.

#### Logical Flow and Cohesion

* Presentation flows in a logical order that aids understanding and retention.
* Connections between sections are unambiguous, making the transition smooth for the audience.
* Assess if the sequence of topics builds on previous information or concepts are introduced abruptly.
* Ensure each section naturally leads to the next, facilitating a coherent learning journey.

#### Practical Applications and Examples

* Includes practical examples and real-world applications to illustrate concepts.
* Demonstrates how theories or strategies are applied in different DS2 job roles.
* Review for inclusion of case studies, scenarios, or examples that relate directly to attendees' daily tasks and roles.
* Evaluate the relevance and effectiveness of examples used to clarify complex concepts.

#### Visual and Multimedia Aids

* Utilizes visual aids (charts, graphs, videos) effectively to complement and enhance the verbal or written information.
* Ensures multimedia is accessible and enhances understanding without distracting from the core content.
* Check for appropriateness and quality of visual aids in supporting the presentation’s messages.
* Ensure multimedia elements are integrated seamlessly and are of high quality to facilitate engagement and understanding.
