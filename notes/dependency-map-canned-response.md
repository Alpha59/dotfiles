### Guidelines for Dependency Maps

#### Objective

The program aim is to create comprehensive Dependency Maps that details technical dependencies and capture the Business Value Stream and Business Process Flow. These maps serve as a shared reference point for both technical and non-technical stakeholders, promoting collaboration and informed decision-making.

#### Key Components

**Core Domain Events**: Highlight pivotal occurrences that initiate actions and orchestrate processes within your domain.
**Hot Spots**: Identify areas requiring special attention or optimization.
**Actors**: Include individuals, systems, or entities that play significant roles in the events.
**Domain Policies**: Specify the rules and regulations governing interactions within the domain.
**Views**: Representations of data or information as it flows through the domain.
**Generic Dependencies**: Common dependencies shared across multiple domains.
**Supporting Domains**: Domains that provide necessary support to the main domain.
**Domain Aggregates**: Groups of related services within a domain.

### Questions to Consider

**Event Identification**: Have you identified and highlighted the core events that drive processes in your domain?
**Hot Spots**: Are there any critical areas or bottlenecks that require optimization?
**Actors and Interactions**: Who are the key actors involved, and how do they interact within the system?
**Policy Inclusion**: Have you documented the domain policies that govern interactions and processes?
**Value Stream**: How does the map reflect the business value stream and process flow?

### Areas to Address

**Visual Clarity**: Ensure that the map is visually clear and easy to navigate for both technical and non-technical users.
**Layered Approach**: Structure the map to offer various levels of detail, from high-level overviews to specific service interactions.
**Dynamic Representation**: Incorporate dynamic elements that help users understand the flow and dependencies within the system.
**Business Integration**: Clearly depict how technical dependencies align with business processes and value streams.
**Review and Update**: Establish a process for regular review and updates to keep the map accurate and relevant.

### Feedback on Current Submissions

**Architectural Focus**: Many maps currently focus heavily on architecture. While this is a good starting point, please ensure that your maps also highlight the business processes and value streams.
**Event Detailing**: Some maps lack detailed representation of core events. Please include these to provide a clearer picture of the processes.
**Actor Roles**: Clearly identify and detail the roles of actors within your domain. This helps in understanding the interactions and dependencies.
**Interactivity**: Consider adding interactive features such as tooltips or links to provide additional information and context.
**Visual Layers**: Implement a layered approach to offer different levels of detail, making it easier for users to navigate and understand the map.

By adhering to these guidelines and addressing the areas mentioned, your Dependency Maps will become more effective tools for collaboration and decision-making.
