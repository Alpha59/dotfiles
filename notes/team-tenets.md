## Tech Team Tenets

Tenets for a software team serve as guiding principles that provide clarity and consistency to team members. They act as a compass, helping team members make decisions and prioritize actions while fostering alignment with the broader organization's goals. Tenets also promote accountability within the team, ensuring adherence to established values. Moreover, they enable adaptability by offering a framework for agile decision-making, ultimately allowing the team to remain resilient and responsive to changing circumstances and challenges.


* **Engineering Leadership:** strong engineering leadership guides our engineering team. Every team member's insights and ideas are valued equally, fostering a culture of collaboration and inclusion.
* **Inclusive Ideation:** We believe that brilliant ideas can originate from any source. To find the best solutions, we start with our in-depth domain knowledge and actively seek diverse opinions to enrich our problem-solving approach.
* **Reverse Logistics Pioneers:** As an engineering team, we take pride in being the foremost experts in the Reverse Logistics domain, representing the interests of Amazon Device customers. Our mission is to lead and innovate in this specialized field.
* **Dependency Management:** We approach new dependencies with caution, recognizing the importance of early and swift onboarding. We uphold the principle that external dependencies should not be expected to make changes on our behalf.
* **Customer-Centric Collaboration:** We acknowledge that other teams rely on us, and this relationship can be challenging. To support our partners, we provide self-service documentation and tooling. When that's insufficient, we embody Customer Obsession in our interactions.
* **Respecting Legacy Systems:** We honor the legacy of our mature systems by learning from them. However, we don't shy away from necessary improvements, even when the existing systems appear to be functioning adequately.
* **Championing Innovation:** Innovation is at the core of our engineering ethos. Our engineers are encouraged to explore new technologies, experiment, and actively contribute insights to drive the planning process, ensuring our team remains at the forefront of technological advancement.

By upholding these tenets in our daily practices and team culture, we solidify our commitment to excellence, innovation, and collaboration, enabling us to tackle complex challenges and deliver exceptional results for Amazon Device customers.

## **Engineering Leadership:**

>**Engineering Leadership:** strong engineering leadership guides our engineering team. Every team member's insights and ideas are valued equally, fostering a culture of collaboration and inclusion.


Engineering leadership is a cornerstone of our team's culture. It signifies that we are guided by experienced and knowledgeable engineering leaders who set the tone for our collaborative efforts. This leadership empowers every team member by ensuring that their opinions, ideas, and contributions are not only heard but valued equally. It matters because it fosters an environment of trust, open communication, and respect among team members. We utilize it daily by actively engaging in discussions, listening attentively to diverse viewpoints, and embracing collective decision-making. To ensure its constant inclusion, we encourage leaders at all levels to lead by example, demonstrating humility, empathy, and a commitment to team success. By doing so, we create a culture where everyone feels empowered to contribute their best.


## **Inclusive Ideation:**

>**Inclusive Ideation:** We believe that brilliant ideas can originate from any source. To find the best solutions, we start with our in-depth domain knowledge and actively seek diverse opinions to enrich our problem-solving approach.


Inclusive ideation reflects our belief that groundbreaking solutions can emerge from any corner of our team. We start our problem-solving journey by leveraging our deep domain knowledge. Still, we actively seek and welcome a broad spectrum of opinions and ideas. This approach matters because it enriches our problem-solving processes with diverse perspectives, leading to innovative, well-rounded solutions. In our daily work, we make space for open discussions, brainstorming sessions, and cross-functional collaborations to encourage inclusive ideation. To ensure its constant inclusion, we maintain an environment where team members feel safe and encouraged to share their thoughts without fear of judgment. We also value constructive criticism as it helps refine ideas and foster continuous improvement.


## **Reverse Logistics Pioneers:**

>**Reverse Logistics Pioneers:** As an engineering team, we take pride in being the foremost experts in the Reverse Logistics domain, representing the interests of Amazon Device customers. Our mission is to lead and innovate in this specialized field.


Being pioneers in the Reverse Logistics domain for Amazon Device customers is both a mission and a responsibility. This tenet underscores our dedication to leading and innovating in this specialized field. It matters because it motivates us to continuously push boundaries, staying at the forefront of industry advancements. In our daily work, we immerse ourselves in the domain, staying informed about the latest trends, challenges, and customer needs. We actively seek opportunities to pioneer new solutions and technologies that enhance our customers' experiences. To ensure its constant inclusion, we promote a culture of learning and curiosity, encouraging team members to stay updated on industry developments and proactively seek innovative solutions to complex problems.


## **Dependency Management:**

>**Dependency Management:** We approach new dependencies with caution, recognizing the importance of early and swift onboarding. We uphold the principle that external dependencies should not be expected to make changes on our behalf.


Prudent management of dependencies is crucial for the smooth functioning of our engineering endeavors. We approach new dependencies cautiously, recognizing that they require early and efficient onboarding. This approach matters because it minimizes disruptions, delays, and misunderstandings in our collaborative efforts. In our daily work, we establish clear communication channels with external teams, facilitate effective handoffs, and provide comprehensive documentation. To ensure its constant inclusion, we emphasize the importance of proactive dependency management in our planning and project timelines, ensuring that we never assume external dependencies will automatically align with our needs. By doing so, we maintain control over our project timelines and ensure a streamlined workflow.


## **Customer-Centric Collaboration:**

>**Customer-Centric Collaboration:** We acknowledge that other teams rely on us, and this relationship can be challenging. To support our partners, we provide self-service documentation and tooling. When that's insufficient, we embody Customer Obsession in our interactions.


Our work doesn't exist in isolation; it's part of a broader ecosystem where other teams rely on our expertise. This tenet underscores our commitment to providing exceptional support to our partners. It matters because it builds trust, fosters healthy relationships, and enables cross-functional collaboration that ultimately benefits our customers. In our daily work, we strive to be responsive and supportive, offering self-service documentation and tooling for our partners. When challenges arise, we model Customer Obsession by prioritizing the customer's needs and going the extra mile to find solutions. To ensure its constant inclusion, we prioritize open and respectful communication with our cross-functional partners. We consider their perspectives and requirements as integral to our decision-making processes.


## **Respecting Legacy Systems:**

>**Respecting Legacy Systems:** We honor the legacy of our mature systems by learning from them. However, we don't shy away from necessary improvements, even when the existing systems appear to be functioning adequately.


Respecting legacy systems acknowledges the value of the knowledge and experience embedded in our mature systems. It also recognizes that we should never settle for the status quo if improvements can be made. This tenet matters because it encourages a balance between honoring our past achievements and striving for continuous enhancement. In our daily work, we critically assess the performance and functionality of our mature systems, identifying areas where improvements are needed. To ensure its constant inclusion, we maintain a culture of constructive feedback and continuous learning. We celebrate the strengths of our legacy systems while remaining open to innovative solutions that may enhance them.


## **Championing Innovation:**

>**Championing Innovation:** Innovation is at the core of our engineering ethos. Our engineers are encouraged to explore new technologies, experiment, and actively contribute insights to drive the planning process, ensuring our team remains at the forefront of technological advancement.


Innovation is the driving force behind our engineering team's success. This tenet embodies our expectation that engineers explore new technologies, experiment, and actively contribute to our planning processes. It matters because it ensures that we remain at the cutting edge of technological advancements, continuously improving our solutions. In our daily work, we encourage engineers to allocate time for experimentation, allowing them to explore new tools, methodologies, and ideas. We value their input in the planning process, welcoming their perspectives on how technology can drive our projects forward. To ensure its constant inclusion, we nurture a culture of curiosity and risk-taking, where team members feel empowered to propose and implement innovative solutions that can transform our work and benefit our customers.
