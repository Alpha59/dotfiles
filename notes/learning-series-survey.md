# Survey to improve Learning Series

### 1. Objective

Identify the reasons for lack of participation, as both presenters and audiences, in the currently running Learning Series. The goal is to use this data to further refine the [proposal](https://quip-amazon.com/tdFyAavCxVrm/DS2-learning-series-proposal) for organizing Learning Series.

### 2. Background

In the last 3-4 months, we have seen no participation in Learning Series. Before that, even when individuals presented a topic, the participating audience consisted mostly from the same team as the presenters. We are working on a new proposal which aims to address some of the challenges that the current learning series faces. However, the challenges identified are based on anecdotal data and guess work. Through this survey, we are reaching out to all the teams in to gather 1) reasons for low participation as both audience and presenter and 2) suggestions to incorporate in the new learning series.

**Question 6: Choose the top 3 recommendations that you think will help increase the value add for people joining the series.**

* Changing the series from weekly to monthly. The goal is to present fewer but high quality presentations on topics which are relevant to the org and provide value add.
* Changing the presentation time from Wed morning to lunch time (12pm - 1pm) according to presenter’s time zone. To cover topics presented in other timezones, we will organize a watch party next week to go over the recorded broadcast presentation.
* Polling members of org to find new topics as well as polling against a fixed set of topics.
* Increasing awareness of topics being presented before the meeting by sending out an abstract of the topic at least 1 week in advance.
