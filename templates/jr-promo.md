# Interviews

I am inviting you to a confidential promotion assessment meeting. During this meeting, we will discuss the professional growth and development of [Name].

The purpose of this assessment is to review contributions, achievements, and potential for advancement within the team/organization. Your insights and feedback are invaluable in this process, and your participation is highly appreciated.

Please come prepared to share your observations, experiences, and perspectives on performance, leadership skills, technical abilities, and any other relevant factors.

Your attendance and candid input will contribute to the decision-making process.

Thank you in advance for your participation.

## Questions

>Describe a recent challenging software problem that the candidate solved effectively, resulting in positive feedback from stakeholders.


This question assesses the candidate's ability to solve difficult problems generating positive feedback, showcasing their problem-solving skills and technical proficiency.
