- [ ] Technical Complexity
    - [ ] Solves Complex Software problems

        * **Complex** problems/efforts have visible risks, roadblocks, and **constraints. **SDE examples: legacy dependencies, data quality, compliance, architecture or technology limitations, extensibility asks, performance, and scalability

    - [ ] Solutions are extensible and scale
    - [ ] Removes bottlenecks
    - [ ] Makes trade-offs: short-term vs. long-term technical decisions
    - [ ] Identify and tackle intrinsically hard problems

        * Major bottleneck or existing architectural complexity that slow down or prevent innovation, pose a critical business or security risk, undefined opportunity with potentially high impact, less prior art to be inspired by

    - [ ] Solves **significantly complex** or endemic software problems

        * **Significantly Complex **problems/efforts have substantial risks/constraints and they **conflict** with each other. Not all constraints or risks are apparent. Requires enough expertise to ‘see around corners’ and to navigate incredibly complicated situations.

    - [ ] Solutions are exemplary in terms of robustness, stability, scalability, cost-effectiveness
    - [ ] Makes trade-offs: opportunity vs. architectural complexity.
