## Summary
**As a** team lead or manager, **I want** to **re-write and define the team tenets and decision criteria**, **so that** the team can have a shared set of guiding principles and clear decision-making criteria that align with our goals and organizational values.

Defining team tenets and decision criteria provides a consistent framework for making decisions, prioritizing work, and guiding team behaviors. These tenets help ensure that the team remains aligned with the broader organizational strategy and can make informed, transparent, and consistent choices.

### Vision
By re-writing and defining the team tenets and decision criteria, the team will have a clear set of principles that guide daily work, decision-making, and long-term planning. This will foster a shared understanding of priorities, improve collaboration, and enhance the team's ability to respond to challenges in a consistent manner.

### Background
Team tenets are the fundamental principles that shape how a team operates, makes decisions, and prioritizes work. Decision criteria provide guidelines for evaluating options and making choices. Over time, these principles may need updating to reflect evolving business priorities, changes in team structure, or new strategic goals.

### Purpose
The purpose of this story is to review the existing team tenets, update them as needed, and define clear decision criteria that will be used to guide prioritization and choices. This ensures that the team has a shared understanding of values and a consistent approach to decision-making.

## Details

### 1. **Review Existing Team Tenets**
   - Conduct a review of the current tenets to determine their relevance and alignment with the team's goals and organizational objectives.
   - Identify any gaps or areas where the existing tenets do not fully support the team's current needs or strategic direction.
   - Gather feedback from team members and stakeholders on which tenets are valuable and which may need revision.

   **Example of Existing Tenets**:
   - **Customer Obsession**: Always prioritize tasks that deliver the most value to customers.
   - **Operational Excellence**: Strive for high availability and minimal downtime.
   - **Data-Driven Decision Making**: Use data to support all major decisions.

### 2. **Define New or Updated Team Tenets**
   - Based on the review, draft new or revised tenets that accurately reflect the team's values, priorities, and long-term goals.
   - Ensure that each tenet is specific, actionable, and easy to understand, providing clear guidance for the team.
   - Limit the number of tenets to five to seven to maintain focus and clarity.

   **Examples of Updated Tenets**:
   - **Customer-Centric Innovation**: Develop features that solve key customer problems while driving innovation.
   - **Continuous Learning and Improvement**: Embrace feedback, iterate on solutions, and continuously improve processes.
   - **Simplicity and Efficiency**: Prefer solutions that simplify workflows and reduce complexity.
   - **Security as a Priority**: Incorporate security considerations in all stages of development.

### 3. **Establish Decision Criteria**
   - Define criteria that the team will use to evaluate and prioritize tasks, projects, and initiatives.
   - Ensure that the criteria align with the tenets and provide a structured approach to decision-making.
   - Create categories of criteria, such as **impact on customers**, **alignment with strategic goals**, **cost and resource availability**, **risk and security considerations**, and **time to market**.

   **Example Decision Criteria**:
   - **Customer Impact**: Prioritize work that significantly improves the customer experience.
   - **Strategic Alignment**: Ensure projects align with the organization's strategic goals and vision.
   - **Effort vs. Benefit**: Evaluate the return on investment for each initiative.
   - **Risk Mitigation**: Consider the potential risks and implement strategies to mitigate them.
   - **Technical Debt**: Prioritize addressing technical debt if it has a significant impact on system performance or development velocity.

### 4. **Facilitate Team Workshop for Feedback and Alignment**
   - Host a workshop or meeting with the team to review the proposed tenets and decision criteria.
   - Gather input from team members to ensure that the tenets resonate with everyone and that the decision criteria are practical.
   - Make any necessary adjustments based on feedback to ensure the final tenets and criteria reflect a consensus.

### 5. **Document the Updated Tenets and Decision Criteria**
   - Create a formal document outlining the updated team tenets and decision criteria.
   - Include examples of how the tenets can be applied in day-to-day work and decision-making scenarios.
   - Distribute the document to the team and make it easily accessible for future reference.

   **Example Document Sections**:
   - **Introduction**: Explanation of the purpose and importance of team tenets and decision criteria.
   - **Updated Tenets**: List each tenet with a description and example use cases.
   - **Decision Criteria**: Outline each criterion with guidelines on how to apply it.
   - **Application Examples**: Provide scenarios showing how to use the tenets and criteria to guide decisions.

### 6. **Integrate Tenets and Criteria into Team Processes**
   - Update relevant team processes (e.g., project planning, sprint planning, code reviews) to incorporate the tenets and decision criteria.
   - Use the tenets as a guiding framework during discussions about trade-offs, project priorities, and resource allocation.
   - Regularly revisit the tenets and criteria to ensure they remain relevant and make adjustments as necessary.

### Testing
- Validate the updated tenets and decision criteria by applying them to recent decisions or upcoming projects.
- Gather feedback from the team to confirm that the tenets provide meaningful guidance.
- Ensure that decision-making is more consistent and aligned with team values after implementation.

### Acceptance Criteria
- Should review and update the team tenets to reflect current priorities and goals.
- Should define clear decision criteria that align with the updated tenets and provide guidance for prioritization.
- Should facilitate a workshop to gather feedback and ensure team alignment.
- Should document the tenets and decision criteria in a formal, accessible format.
- Should integrate the tenets and criteria into relevant team processes.
