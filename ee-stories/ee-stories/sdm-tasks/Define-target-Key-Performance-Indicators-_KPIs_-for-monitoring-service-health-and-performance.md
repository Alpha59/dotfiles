## Summary
**As a** service operations manager, **I want** to define target Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) **so that** we can effectively monitor the health and performance of our service and make data-driven decisions to improve it.

### Vision
The vision for this story is to establish a set of meaningful and actionable KPIs that directly correlate with service health and performance. These metrics will provide insights into operational effectiveness and guide strategic improvements.

### Background
Currently, our service monitoring lacks specific metrics that can accurately reflect the true health and performance of the service. This deficiency hampers our ability to proactively manage and optimize service delivery.

### Purpose
The purpose of defining target KPIs is to enhance our monitoring capabilities, which will enable us to quickly identify areas needing improvement, assess the impact of changes, and ensure that the service meets both business and customer expectations.

## Details
1. Identify critical aspects of service health and performance that are vital for operational success.
2. Develop a list of potential KPIs that measure these aspects, such as uptime, response time, error rates, customer satisfaction scores, and resource utilization.
3. Collaborate with stakeholders to validate and prioritize KPIs based on their relevance and impact on service quality.
4. Define targets for each KPI that align with industry standards and business objectives.
5. Implement monitoring tools and processes to regularly capture these metrics.
6. Plan regular review meetings to assess KPI performance and make necessary adjustments.

### Testing
- Test the monitoring tools and systems to ensure accurate tracking and reporting of KPIs.
- Conduct a pilot period to refine KPI thresholds and targets based on initial performance data.

### External Dependencies
- Coordination with IT to set up and configure monitoring tools.
- Input from business analysts to ensure alignment of KPIs with business goals.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Should have a defined list of KPIs with clear targets for each metric.
- Should have implemented monitoring tools that provide real-time data on these KPIs.
- Should have a dashboard that visualizes these KPIs for easy interpretation and access.
- Should have conducted a review session with stakeholders to validate the effectiveness of the KPIs.

### Gherkin
