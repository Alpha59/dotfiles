## Summary
**As a** team manager, **I want** information about the on-call group and "Contact Us" links added to the team operational dashboard **so that** team members and stakeholders can quickly contact the right support channels during operational issues.

### Vision
The vision for this story is to enhance the operational effectiveness of the team dashboard by providing immediate access to essential communication resources. This will enable faster resolution of issues by facilitating direct contact with the on-call support group and other relevant contacts.

### Background
Currently, team members and stakeholders experience delays when attempting to identify and contact the on-call support group during critical incidents, as this information is not readily accessible from the team dashboard.

### Purpose
The purpose of adding on-call group details and "Contact Us" links to the dashboard is to provide instant access to support resources, reducing the time needed to initiate contact during operational incidents and improving overall response times.

## Details
1. Compile a list of on-call group members and their contact details.
2. Design and implement a section on the dashboard for displaying this information clearly and accessibly.
3. Include a "Contact Us" link that directs users to a contact form or directly opens an email or chat window.
4. Ensure all contact links and information are kept up to date through an automated process or regular manual updates.

### Testing
- Test the visibility and accessibility of the on-call information and "Contact Us" links on different devices.
- Conduct user acceptance tests to ensure the information is accurate and the links function as intended.

### External Dependencies
- Coordination with the IT and communications teams to verify contact details and ensure system compatibility.
- Regular updates from HR to maintain current on-call group information.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Should have displayed all current on-call group members’ names, roles, and contact details prominently on the dashboard.
- Should have the "Contact Us" link open a communication channel directly, such as email or chat, with pre-populated address or subject line.
- Should have ensured all information and links are responsive and accessible on mobile devices.
- Should have tested with end-users for clarity and accessibility, receiving at least 90% positive feedback.

### Gherkin
Given a user is on the team operational dashboard,
When they need to contact the on-call support group,
Then they should find accurate contact details readily available and be able to initiate contact through the "Contact Us" link without navigating away from the dashboard.

## API
No API changes are proposed in this story.
