## Summary
**As a** product manager, **I want** to clearly define the purpose and functionality of our service **so that** customers can easily understand what the service offers and how it benefits them.

### Vision
The vision of this story is to create comprehensive, clear, and easily accessible documentation and marketing material that elucidates the purpose and functionality of our service. This will empower customers by providing them with the knowledge they need to effectively utilize our service, ensuring they receive the full value of their investment.

### Background
Currently, feedback indicates that customers are sometimes unclear about the full range of functionalities offered by our service or how to utilize them effectively. This confusion can lead to underutilization of the service and dissatisfaction.

### Purpose
The purpose of this initiative is to enhance customer satisfaction and engagement by delivering clear, concise, and helpful information about our service. By educating customers on how our service can solve their problems and add value to their operations, we can improve retention rates, attract new customers, and build a stronger brand.

## Details
1. Identify key functionalities of the service that need clearer communication.
2. Develop content that describes the purpose of the service and details each functionality, including use cases and benefits.
3. Utilize various formats such as FAQs, tutorial videos, infographics, and user manuals to cater to different learning preferences.
4. Ensure all communications are jargon-free and use layman’s terms to enhance understanding.
5. Collaborate with marketing to distribute content through multiple channels such as the company website, social media, email newsletters, and customer portals.

### Testing
- Gather feedback from a focus group of existing and potential customers on the clarity and usefulness of the information.
- Revise content based on feedback to ensure it meets customer needs and expectations.

### External Dependencies
- Collaboration with the marketing team to align the communication strategy with overall brand messaging.
- Coordination with customer service to update training materials and scripts based on the new content.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Should have developed content that clearly defines the purpose and each key functionality of the service.
- Should have content available in at least three different formats to accommodate various user preferences.
- Should have feedback from customers indicating improved understanding and satisfaction with the information provided.
- Should have all new materials reviewed and approved by the legal department to ensure compliance with advertising standards.

### Gherkin
Given a customer accesses information about the service,
When they read the documentation or view the materials,
Then they should be able to articulate what the service does and how it can be utilized in their context without further explanation.
