## Summary
**As a** project lead, **I want** an architecture diagram **so that** I can facilitate easy discussions with business partners and clarify technical dependencies.

### Vision
The vision of this story is to create a visual representation that clearly outlines the architecture of our current project. This diagram will serve as a vital tool for discussions, ensuring that both technical and non-technical stakeholders can easily understand system components, their interactions, and dependencies. By doing so, we aim to enhance collaboration, accelerate decision-making, and align project goals across teams.

### Background
Currently, our project discussions with business partners and internal teams can be challenging due to the lack of a unified understanding of the entire system architecture. This complexity often leads to miscommunication and inefficiencies in planning and execution.

### Purpose
The purpose of creating an architecture diagram is to provide a clear, visual explanation of how different parts of the system interact. This will help in identifying potential issues early, align business and technical strategies, and facilitate smoother integration and scalability planning.

## Details
1. Identify all system components and their functionalities.
2. Clarify relationships and dependencies between components.
3. Use standardized symbols and notations (e.g., UML or ERD) for clarity and consistency.
4. Include high-level data flows and interaction points.
5. Consult with technical leads to ensure accuracy and completeness.

### Testing
- Review the diagram with technical leads for accuracy.
- Present the diagram in a stakeholder meeting to ensure it meets the needs for understanding and decision-making.

### External Dependencies
- Inputs from system architects and developers on the current system design.
- Feedback from business analysts to ensure business requirements are accurately represented.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Should have identified all primary system components and their interactions.
- Should have used consistent and standardized notations for the diagram.
- Should have verified the diagram for accuracy with at least three technical leads.
- Should have presented the diagram in at least one business partner meeting and incorporated feedback.
