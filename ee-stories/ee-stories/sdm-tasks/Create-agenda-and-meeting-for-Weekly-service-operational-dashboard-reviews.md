## Weekly Service Operational Dashboard Review Meeting

### Purpose
To review the service’s operational dashboard, assess key metrics, address anomalies, and plan necessary actions to maintain service health, reliability, and performance.

---

### Meeting Details
- **Frequency**: Weekly
- **Duration**: 60 minutes
- **Participants**:
  - Service Owner
  - On-Call Engineer(s)
  - SRE/Operations Team
  - Product Manager (if applicable)
  - Observability Lead
- **Location**: Virtual (Zoom/Teams) or Conference Room
- **Facilitator**: Service Owner or Assigned Lead

---

### Agenda

#### 1. **Opening and Context Setting (5 minutes)**
- Facilitator welcomes participants.
- Provide a brief overview of the purpose and objectives of the meeting.

#### 2. **Review of Key Metrics and Alerts (20 minutes)**
- **Performance Metrics**:
  - Service latency (p50, p95, p99).
  - API error rates (4xx, 5xx).
  - Throughput (requests per second).
- **Resource Utilization**:
  - CPU, memory, and disk usage.
  - Database query performance.
  - Queue depth and processing time.
- **Incidents and Alerts**:
  - Review alerts triggered in the past week.
  - Discuss their resolutions and learnings.

#### 3. **Customer Usage and Trends (10 minutes)**
- Highlight top resource-consuming customers.
- Analyze customer usage trends (e.g., API calls, storage utilization).
- Identify potential capacity bottlenecks or anomalies.

#### 4. **Follow-Up on Action Items (10 minutes)**
- Review action items from the previous meeting.
- Provide updates on in-progress tasks.

#### 5. **Identify and Prioritize New Action Items (10 minutes)**
- Discuss areas of improvement based on metrics and trends.
- Assign owners and set deadlines for action items.

#### 6. **Closing Remarks (5 minutes)**
- Summarize key takeaways and decisions.
- Confirm the agenda and facilitator for the next meeting.
- Open the floor for final comments or concerns.

---

### Pre-Meeting Preparation
- **Facilitator**:
  - Prepare the dashboard and ensure all metrics are updated.
  - Highlight critical points or anomalies to discuss.
- **Participants**:
  - Review the dashboard and metrics beforehand.
  - Bring insights or questions related to your area of responsibility.

---

### Post-Meeting Actions
- **Meeting Notes**:
  - Document key discussions, decisions, and action items.
  - Share notes with participants and stakeholders within 24 hours.
- **Action Item Tracking**:
  - Use a tracking tool (e.g., Jira, Asana) to monitor progress.
  - Review outstanding items in the next meeting.

---

This agenda ensures a structured, productive discussion of the operational dashboard, aligning the team on priorities and actions to maintain optimal service performance.
