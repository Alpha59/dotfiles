## Summary
**As a** product team, **I want** to identify key features and capabilities of our service **so that** we can effectively communicate these to customers, highlighting how our service meets their needs and stands out in the marketplace.

### Vision
The vision for this story is to pinpoint the distinct features and capabilities of our service that deliver significant value to customers. This clarity will enable our marketing and sales teams to communicate more effectively, ensuring that customers fully understand the benefits and functionalities of our service.

### Background
Feedback from sales and customer support indicates that potential and existing customers may not be fully aware of all the unique features and capabilities our service offers. This lack of awareness can impact their decision-making process and overall satisfaction with the service.

### Purpose
The purpose of identifying and clarifying the key features and capabilities of our service is to enhance customer engagement and satisfaction. By effectively communicating what our service offers, we aim to differentiate our product in the market, increase sales, and improve customer retention.

## Details
1. Conduct a thorough review of the service to catalog all current features and capabilities.
2. Collaborate with product development, marketing, and customer support teams to determine which features provide the most value based on customer usage data and feedback.
3. Prioritize features and capabilities for communication based on their uniqueness, customer interest, and competitive advantage.
4. Prepare detailed descriptions for each key feature and capability, explaining their benefits and real-world applications.
5. Develop messaging guidelines for these features to ensure consistency across all marketing and communication channels.

### Testing
- Validate the selected key features with a focus group of customers to ensure they resonate and meet customer needs.
- Use A/B testing in marketing campaigns to measure customer response and refine messaging based on performance data.

### External Dependencies
- Collaboration with the customer insight and analytics teams to obtain and analyze customer usage and satisfaction data.
- Regular updates from product development to ensure the features list remains current with any new capabilities or enhancements.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Should have a finalized list of key features and capabilities that are verified as high-value by customer feedback.
- Should have comprehensive descriptions for each feature that clearly articulates its benefits and usage.
- Should have conducted customer focus groups to validate the relevance and appeal of these key features.
- Should have updated all marketing materials to include these key features and capabilities consistently.
