## Summary
**As a** team lead or manager, **I want** to explore and document **expendable or subsidized training opportunities** relevant to team members, **so that** the team can leverage company-sponsored resources to improve their skills and remain current with industry trends.

Providing access to training opportunities allows team members to develop their technical and professional skills, enhancing both individual and team performance. By documenting available training programs, we ensure that everyone has access to the tools they need to grow.

### Vision
By identifying and documenting company-funded or subsidized training programs, team members can pursue opportunities for skill enhancement in areas like software development, cloud technologies, DevOps, security, and leadership. This fosters a culture of continuous improvement and helps the team stay competitive in the fast-evolving tech landscape.

### Background
Many companies offer training budgets or subsidized learning programs to their employees. These may include certifications, online courses, workshops, or professional development programs. However, team members may be unaware of these opportunities or how to access them.

### Purpose
The purpose of this story is to explore all available company-sponsored training programs and document relevant opportunities for team members, ensuring they are aware of the resources available to them for professional development.

## Details

### Steps:
1. **Research Available Training Programs**: Identify company-sponsored or subsidized training platforms (e.g., LinkedIn Learning, Coursera, Udemy, AWS Training).
2. **Determine Relevant Courses**: List trainings applicable to key team roles (e.g., cloud certifications, software engineering, leadership).
3. **Define Application Process**: Document how team members can apply for training, including approval processes, if necessary.
4. **Budget Allocation**: Confirm any individual or team budget allocations for training purposes.

### Testing
- Ensure documentation is clear and shared with the team.
- Validate that team members can easily access and apply for training resources.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Should document all available training programs and resources.
- Should provide information on accessing and applying for training opportunities.
- Should ensure relevant training is highlighted for each team role.
