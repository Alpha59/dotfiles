## Summary
**As a** project manager or team lead, **I want** to plan and host a **quarterly stakeholder retrospective** to review issues, discrepancies, and pain points, **so that** stakeholders and team members can collaboratively address concerns, identify solutions, and improve processes for future projects.

A quarterly retrospective offers an opportunity for stakeholders to reflect on what went well and what didn't, ensuring that issues are addressed and improvements are made for the next quarter. It promotes transparency, collaboration, and a culture of continuous improvement by identifying and resolving discrepancies or bottlenecks in processes, communication, or deliverables.

### Vision
By hosting quarterly retrospectives with stakeholders, the team will create an open forum to review project performance, identify and resolve issues, and streamline workflows. These retrospectives will foster alignment between stakeholders and the development team, improving overall efficiency and satisfaction with project outcomes.

### Background
Throughout each quarter, various challenges, misalignments, or pain points may arise, affecting the smooth progress of projects. Without regular reviews, these issues can compound and reduce productivity or stakeholder satisfaction. A structured retrospective ensures that stakeholders have a voice in the improvement process and that actions are taken to resolve identified issues.

### Purpose
The purpose of this story is to plan and facilitate a quarterly stakeholder retrospective to gather feedback on ongoing projects, discuss any challenges or concerns, and create actionable steps for improvement in future work. This will help foster continuous improvement and ensure alignment between stakeholders and the development team.

## Details

### 1. **Define Objectives and Agenda**
   - **Objective**: The primary objective of the retrospective is to gather feedback from stakeholders about the previous quarter's performance, discuss issues or discrepancies, and plan improvements for the next quarter.

   **Sample Objectives**:
   - Identify key issues or blockers that impacted project timelines or outcomes.
   - Highlight successes and best practices that should be continued.
   - Discuss discrepancies in project goals, expectations, or communication.
   - Create actionable steps to address pain points and improve future collaboration.

   **Agenda**:
   - **Welcome and Introduction** (5 minutes): Set the tone for an open and constructive discussion.
   - **Review of Previous Quarter** (10-15 minutes): Present key deliverables, achievements, and issues from the past quarter.
   - **Discussion of Issues and Discrepancies** (20-30 minutes): Open the floor for stakeholders to discuss pain points, discrepancies, or blockers they experienced.
   - **Action Items and Solutions** (20-30 minutes): Collaboratively brainstorm and agree on solutions for the identified issues. Assign action items with owners and deadlines.
   - **Closing and Next Steps** (5-10 minutes): Summarize key takeaways, next steps, and thank attendees for their participation.

### 2. **Invite Stakeholders and Team Members**
   - Identify the key stakeholders who were involved in the projects during the quarter. This may include:
     - Project managers
     - Development team leads
     - Product owners
     - Marketing, sales, or customer success teams
     - External partners or vendors
   - Send invitations at least 2-3 weeks in advance to ensure maximum attendance. Provide an agenda and objectives in the invitation to set expectations.

   **Sample Invitation**:
   ```
   Subject: Invitation to Quarterly Stakeholder Retrospective - [Date]

   Dear [Name],

   You are invited to our Quarterly Stakeholder Retrospective to discuss the progress, challenges, and outcomes of our recent projects. The goal of the retrospective is to gather feedback, address any pain points, and collaboratively plan for improvements moving forward.

   Date: [Date]
   Time: [Time]
   Location: [Physical/Virtual Meeting Link]

   Agenda:
   - Welcome and Introduction
   - Review of Previous Quarter's Deliverables
   - Discussion of Issues, Discrepancies, and Pain Points
   - Action Items and Solutions
   - Closing and Next Steps

   Your insights and feedback are invaluable to improving our processes, and we hope you can join us. Please let us know if you have any questions or topics you'd like to add to the agenda.

   Best regards,
   [Your Name]
   ```

### 3. **Collect Pre-Retrospective Feedback**
   - Send out a brief survey or feedback form to all stakeholders a week before the retrospective to gather their thoughts on key issues or topics they want to discuss. This ensures that all voices are heard, even if they can't attend the meeting.

   **Sample Feedback Questions**:
   - What challenges or blockers did you encounter this quarter?
   - Were there any discrepancies between your expectations and project outcomes?
   - What processes or practices worked well, and should we continue?
   - Do you have any suggestions for improvement?

   Tools like **Google Forms**, **SurveyMonkey**, or **Microsoft Forms** can be used to gather this feedback.

### 4. **Host the Retrospective**
   - During the retrospective, follow the agenda closely to ensure that the discussion remains productive and focused.
   - Encourage open and respectful communication. Make sure all stakeholders have the opportunity to speak, and facilitate the conversation if necessary.
   - Use a virtual whiteboard (e.g., **Miro**, **MURAL**, or **Google Jamboard**) or physical whiteboard to capture ideas, issues, and proposed solutions.

   **Key Facilitation Tips**:
   - Keep the conversation balanced, giving everyone a chance to share their feedback.
   - Focus on solutions and action items rather than dwelling on problems.
   - Ensure action items are assigned to specific individuals and have clear deadlines.

### 5. **Document Key Takeaways and Action Items**
   - After the retrospective, compile a summary document that includes:
     - **Key Discussion Points**: A summary of the issues and pain points raised by stakeholders.
     - **Action Items**: A list of agreed-upon actions to address each issue, including assigned owners and deadlines.
     - **Next Steps**: What the team will do to implement the action items and improve for the next quarter.

   **Sample Action Item Template**:
   | Issue/Discrepancy | Action Item | Owner | Deadline |
   |-------------------|-------------|-------|----------|
   | Project delays due to unclear requirements | Define a more structured requirements-gathering process | Jane Doe | End of Q2 |
   | Miscommunication between development and marketing teams | Set up regular check-ins between teams | John Smith | Ongoing |

   Share this document with all stakeholders within a few days after the retrospective.

### 6. **Follow Up on Action Items**
   - Track the progress of action items throughout the following quarter. Schedule regular check-ins with the owners of each action item to ensure they are completed.
   - During the next retrospective, review the progress on action items from the previous meeting to ensure that improvements were implemented.

### Testing
- Test the effectiveness of the retrospective by gathering feedback from stakeholders afterward. Ask if they felt the meeting was productive, if their concerns were heard, and if they see improvements based on the discussion.
- Review the completion of action items throughout the next quarter to measure the effectiveness of the proposed solutions.

### Acceptance Criteria
- Should define clear objectives and a structured agenda for the quarterly retrospective.
- Should invite all relevant stakeholders and team members at least 2-3 weeks before the meeting.
- Should collect pre-retrospective feedback to ensure that all key issues are discussed.
- Should host the retrospective in a productive and open manner, allowing stakeholders to voice their concerns.
- Should document key takeaways, assign action items with owners and deadlines, and distribute them to all stakeholders.
- Should follow up on action items during the next quarter to ensure improvements are implemented.
